🤔 General GPU Buying Questions

Which GPU is best for 1080p gaming?

NVIDIA RTX 4060 or AMD Radeon RX 6600 - both deliver excellent 1080p performance with high refresh rates. The 4060 has DLSS 3, while the 6600 is slightly cheaper.

Which GPU has more VRAM?

Intel ARC B580 leads with 12GB VRAM, followed by RTX 3060 (12GB), then all others at 8GB. More VRAM helps with future-proofing and professional workloads.

Which GPU is best for content creation?

NVIDIA RTX 4060 or A750 - both support AV1 encoding for excellent video editing quality, while RTX 4060 has CUDA cores for AI workloads.
